🔒 Privacy Policy

SkillPath: IT Career Roadmap Tracker is committed to transparent data handling and user privacy.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account and interact with SkillPath, we store the minimum necessary data to provide personalized learning roadmap and progress tracking services:

  • Full Name & Email Address: Used for account identity, login credentials, password resets, and session authorization via Supabase Auth.
  • Account Role: Assigned as either user (default student) or admin (curriculum administrator), governing platform permissions.
  • Roadmap Task Progress: Which specific learning tasks you mark as completed, including timestamps in public.user_progress.
  • Personal Roadmaps & Tasks: Any custom domains, milestones, and tasks you manually create under your account (is_global = false).
  • Weekly Study Planner & Resources: Any tasks entered into your weekly study planner or saved bookmarks in your resources directory.

2. How Your Data Is Secured (Row Level Security)

We enforce strict PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) directly on database tables:

  • Your personal domains and custom tasks are private to your user ID and cannot be viewed, altered, or deleted by other users.
  • Your completion checkmarks are linked directly to your authenticated session.
  • Database security triggers block non-admin accounts from modifying their own roles.
  • Passwords are cryptographically hashed and never stored or visible in plain text.

3. Global Roadmaps vs. Personal Roadmaps

SkillPath features two types of roadmaps:

  • Global Roadmaps: Curated career tracks published by SkillPath administrators (e.g. AI Engineering, Full Stack Development). All authenticated users can view these roadmaps and track their individual progress.
  • Personal Roadmaps: Custom career paths you create yourself. Only you can view, edit, or delete your personal domains and tasks.

4. Third-Party Services

SkillPath runs as a static client hosted on GitHub Pages or Vercel, utilizing Supabase for authentication, database storage, and Row Level Security. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers.

5. Contact & Data Deletion

If you wish to export your roadmap data, use the 💾 Export feature in the dashboard navigation. To request account or data removal, please contact the repository administrator.
